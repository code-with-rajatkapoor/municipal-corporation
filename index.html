Healthy city guidance

🧹 Safety & Cleanliness

Simple actions residents can take to keep streets, drains, water areas and public spaces safe and clean.

🗑️

Manage household waste

Keep wet and dry waste separate, use covered bins and hand waste to the authorized collection team.

  • Separate wet and dry waste
  • Do not throw waste on roads
  • Keep bins covered
🚰

Protect water & drains

Keep drains free from plastic and waste, report leaks quickly and avoid dumping chemicals into storm drains.

  • Report leaks and overflow
  • Keep drain openings clear
  • Do not pour oils or chemicals into drains
🛣️

Keep streets safe

Report potholes, fallen trees, damaged covers, blocked roads and other hazards with a clear location.

  • Use the map pin when reporting
  • Add a photo where safe
  • Keep emergency access routes clear
🧼

Clean public spaces

Use public toilets responsibly, avoid littering and support regular cleaning around markets, parks and community areas.

  • Use designated bins
  • Do not spit in public places
  • Keep shared areas clean
